Questions.

What does the free dojo include?
The real game — both round types, the Strike, the Gauntlet, the Unfurling, the Council, standings and the closing ceremony — for up to 8 samurai on the starter prompts. No card, no trial clock.
What do we need to run it?
A laptop with a browser for the stage, a screen everyone can see (TV, projector, or a shared tab in Google Meet), and each player's phone. Nothing to install.
How many people can play?
The dojo holds 5 to 15 comfortably; a paid night supports up to 30. Below five, the guessing gets thin. Above thirty, run two dojos and crown a Shogun of Shoguns.
Does it work for remote teams?
Yes — that's dual control. Share the stage tab in Meet, Zoom or Teams, and everyone plays from their phone wherever they are. Practice samurai can fill an empty seat while people trickle in.
What happens to what people write?
By default nothing is stored — answers live for the night and are gone when the dojo closes. Premium plans with standings history are opt-in, controlled by your organization, and deletable at any time. See the privacy policy.
Can we write our own prompts?
On Premium, yes — your own scroll and council library with an approval step, so nothing reaches the big screen that shouldn't. The stock bank of 100 is included on every plan.
