How a night works

Cast it. Join it. Draw swords.

Social Samurai runs like a game show, not a meeting. Everyone writes their answers upfront — then the sensei walks the room through the reveals, one secret at a time, while everyone accuses, bluffs, and locks in guesses from their phone. Concealment scoring means not being guessed earns points too, so the quiet ones are dangerous.

一Put the stage on the big screen

Open the game on a laptop and cast to the TV or projector — or share the tab in your video call. The stage runs the show: intro sequence, live scores, reveals.

二Everyone joins with a room code

Players open a link on their phone and type the six-letter code on screen. No app store, no accounts, no IT ticket. Eight to thirty players.

三Write once, upfront

Everyone answers the night's prompts before round one — so there's zero dead air mid-game, and nobody's phone anxiety holds up the room.

四Seven rounds of reveals

A secret hits the stage, the room shouts, phones lock in guesses, the card flips. Right guesses build streaks; staying hidden builds concealment bonuses. Crowning happens at the end.

Dual control

Runs right inside Google Meet.

Remote and hybrid teams don't get the second-class version — the Meet is the big screen. The sensei shares the stage tab and drives the night; every player plays from their phone.

Questions

Asked before drawing.

What does the free version include?
The real game — full rounds, scoring, the works — for up to 8 players with the starter question pack. No credit card, no trial clock. When you want 30 players, the full bank, or custom questions, that's a Premium thing.
What do we need to run it?
A laptop with a browser, a screen everyone can see (TV, projector, or a shared tab in Google Meet), and each player's phone. That's the whole stack.
How many players does it support?
Eight players is the sweet spot; paid nights support up to 30. Larger than that, split into two rooms and crown a champion of champions.
Does it work for remote and hybrid teams?
Yes — that's the dual-control design: share the stage in Google Meet (or Zoom, or Teams — it's a browser tab) and everyone plays from their phone wherever they are.
What happens to what people write?
By default, nothing is stored — answers live for the night and are gone when the room closes. On Premium plans with game history, your organization controls retention and can delete everything at any time. Details in our privacy policy.
What if something breaks during our event?
If a technical failure on our side ends a paid game night, we refund that night. No form, no argument.
