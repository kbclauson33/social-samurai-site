How a night works

Cast it. Join it. Draw swords.

Social Samurai runs like a game show, not a meeting. The big screen is the stage; everyone's phone is their sword. No downloads, no accounts for players, no one reading instructions aloud for ten minutes.

一 · ONE

Put the stage on the big screen

Open the game on a laptop and cast it to the TV or projector — office, offsite, or a shared screen on the video call.

二 · TWO

Everyone joins with a room code

Players open a link on their phone and type the code on screen. Eight to thirty players, in the room or remote.

三 · THREE

The sensei runs seven rounds

Everyone answers upfront — then the game reveals answers one by one while the room bluffs, accuses, and guesses who wrote what. Concealment scoring means hiding well earns points too, so nobody checks out.

Why it lands

Built to make coworkers interesting to each other.

◦Nobody sits out

Concealment scoring rewards you for not being guessed, so quiet people score as hard as loud ones. Writing happens upfront, so there's no dead air mid-game.

◦Zero setup for players

Phones join through the browser with a room code. No app store, no accounts, no IT ticket.

◦Production value included

An opening title sequence, a live score stage, procedurally drawn player crests and an original score generated in real time. It feels like an event, because it is one.

◦Questions that do the work

A curated bank of 80 prompts tuned for coworkers — revealing without being risky. On the Company plan, write your own: inside jokes are the whole point.

Questions

Asked before drawing.

What do we need to run it?
A laptop with a browser, a screen everyone can see (TV, projector, or a shared screen on your video call), and each player's phone. That's the whole stack.
How many players does it support?
Eight players is the sweet spot; a Single Event night supports up to 30. Larger than that, split into two rooms and crown a champion of champions.
Does it work for remote and hybrid teams?
Yes. Share the stage screen on your video call and everyone joins from their phone, wherever they are. It was built for exactly this.
What happens to what people write?
By default, nothing is stored — answers live for the night and are gone when the room closes. On plans with game history, your organization controls retention and can delete everything at any time. Details in our privacy policy.
Can we write our own questions?
On the Company plan, yes — your own question library, with an approval step so nothing reaches the big screen that shouldn't. The stock bank of 80 is included on every plan.
What if something breaks during our event?
If a technical failure on our side ends a paid event, we refund that night. No form, no argument.
