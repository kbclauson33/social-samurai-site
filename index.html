QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

How many people can play?
A dojo holds 5–15 samurai per game. That’s the sweet spot where guessing stays hard and the Unfurling stays fun. Bigger org? Run parallel dojos.
Does it work in Google Meet? Zoom? Teams?
Yes, all three. The host shares the Dojo Stage as a tab — with tab audio for the dojo music, if you like. Players answer on their own phones, so nothing about your call changes.
Is it really safe for work?
Every prompt was stress-tested against a cast of personas and screened for politics, religion, and anything embarrassing. Questions surface stories — “your first job”, “a skill that never comes up at work” — never secrets. The host can also reroll any question live.
Do players need accounts?
No. Players type a dojo code and their name. Ten seconds, any phone or laptop. Only the host has an account.
What are the prizes?
Whatever you set. The ceremony ships with gold 金, silver 銀, and bronze 銅 slots — default suggestions like “$60 on Amazon” or “any drink you like, delivered today”. Premium lets you customize every award.
